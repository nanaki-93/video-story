The character profile already provides a contact sheet: front, side, back and three-quarter turnaround, expressions, proportions and palette. It shows a large rounded lavender/pink axolotl head, branching pink frills, yellow forehead star/freckles, pink/dark headphones, green coat with gold patterned trim, short body, broad tail and small bare paws. Preserve these observed features. The seated train reference retains them in a three-quarter composition with a warm window light, a foreground table, book and takeaway cup. The hand-under-chin pose and table occlusion belong to that seated composition; they are not an interchangeable neutral rig.

Palette samples in the evidence are five-by-five pixel means at documented coordinates in the original profile swatch row. They describe these raster pixels, including texture/shading. They are not an approved flat-color specification or a color-managed paint master.

The editable source delivery needs these aligned groups on the chosen scene canvas:

| Group | Required preparation and review |
| --- | --- |
| Tail and rear body | Preserve the seated silhouette; complete hidden seat/head/arm regions |
| Torso and coat | Local breathing deformation; keep face/head independent |
| Head and frills | Fixed attachment points; complete areas exposed by looking outside |
| Eyes and lids | Open/half/closed states with an independent face channel |
| Arms, paws and headphones | Layer order, pivots and interaction poses matched to the scene |
| Props | Separate book/cup geometry; explicit table/hand ownership and occlusion |

The supplied files are flattened RGB sources, and the transparent action frames remain flattened character composites. No PSD/ORA/KRA/SVG/XCF or equivalent editable rig was found. Wrapping a bitmap in a vector file would not supply the missing editable pieces. Separated masters, hidden-region repair and motion approval therefore remain outstanding; no new character design or invented layer geometry is substituted.

The current plan starts from these supplied references and the existing outfit/action/scenery library. Verify the exact selected source hashes when preparing each new pack. That selection alone does not approve later cutouts, animation, rights, or publication. Once source selection and prepared layers exist, review alignment, alpha on light/dark backgrounds, pivots, face attachment and transition endpoints against these originals. Production approval applies to the final pack hash only.
